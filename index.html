Rashod Underwood is a Los Angeles-based writer, creator, and producer whose journey from the football fields of Sacred Heart University to the writers room represents exactly the kind of authentic, unlikely American story he puts on screen.

Born and raised in Baltimore, Underwood grew up inside the world of professional comedy. His cousin and mentor, Reggie Carroll, was a veteran Hollywood comedian and career ghostwriter — with credits including Showtime at the Apollo, BET's Comic View, The Parkers, and Moesha — and whose behind-the-scenes work spanned over twenty years writing material for some of stand-up comedy's biggest names, including Katt Williams. It was Mo'Nique — a Baltimore native and longtime collaborator of Carroll's — who influenced Carroll to make the move from Baltimore to Los Angeles. Underwood grew up watching two Baltimore kids build careers from nothing in the same city he came from. That proximity — to the craft, to the hustle, to the relationships — is the education that shaped him.

After graduating from Sacred Heart University with a business degree — where he competed as a Division I football captain and lacrosse player — he relocated to Los Angeles and spent years working at the corporate level while his creative voice developed alongside it. The discipline of athletics and the rigor of business gave him something rare in the creative space: he understands both the art and the economics of what he's building.

In 2022, he wrote and produced his first original pilot, Rent & Go — a single-camera mockumentary comedy about a family-owned rental car company in South Los Angeles — and shot a proof-of-concept trailer. That project is now in the WME pipeline and on The Black List.

The slate that followed reflects a creator who thinks in terms of a body of work, not a single shot. Each project is in active development with scripts, series bibles, and pitch decks complete. Twin Vision is named for my twins.

The work is done. The voice is formed. The room is the only thing left.
